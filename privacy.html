Cardly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Cardly is a flashcard app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your cards stay on your phone. Cardly has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we never sell or share your data.

Information stored on your device

Everything you create or add in Cardly stays on your device: your decks, cards, study history, your first name if you give one, your learning goals and your reminder time. This data is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Backups and imports

When you tap Back up now, Cardly creates a file on your phone and opens Android’s share menu. You choose where the file goes, for example Google Drive or email. That service’s own privacy policy then applies. Cardly never uploads backups anywhere by itself.

When you import a backup, a CSV file or an Anki deck, Cardly reads only the file you pick and only on your device.

Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, Cardly asks for permission to show notifications and schedules them on your device. No push service is used and no notification token is created.

App updates

Cardly receives small fixes through Expo’s update service (EAS Update, run by 650 Industries, Inc.). To check for an update, the app sends Expo non-identifying technical details: your operating system, the app’s version and project ID, and a random identifier created when the app is installed. Like any web request, this also reveals your IP address to Expo’s servers. None of it includes your name, email or cards, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. See Expo’s privacy policy.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect personal information such as your name, email, contacts or location.
  • We don’t show ads or use advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We don’t sell, rent or share data with third parties.

Children

Cardly is a general study app and isn’t aimed at children under 13. It doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Your choices

You can delete any deck or card in the app, reset your study progress in Settings, or remove all data by uninstalling Cardly. Because we hold no data about you on our side, there is nothing further to request or delete.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Cardly adds features such as optional accounts or sync, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date at the top.
